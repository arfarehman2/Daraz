How to Buy

Steps to Buy Products on Daraz

  1. Visit the Daraz website or open the Daraz app.
  2. Search for the product you want to buy.
  3. Click on the product to view its details.
  4. Click on "Add to Cart" to add the item to your cart.
  5. Proceed to checkout and enter your shipping and payment information.
  6. Review your order and confirm the purchase.
